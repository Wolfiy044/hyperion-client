Privacy Policy

Applies to the Hyperion Client launcher for Windows.

What the launcher stores on your computer

Profiles, settings, downloaded game files, logs and screenshots stay on your computer in your user folder. They are not uploaded to us.

Microsoft sign-in

To verify that you own Minecraft, Hyperion Client uses the official Microsoft, Xbox and Minecraft sign-in flow. You enter your password only on Microsoft pages, never inside Hyperion Client. The sign-in tokens are stored on your computer and are used only to start your game and to check ownership. We do not receive or store your Microsoft password or tokens on any server of ours.

Network requests

  • Mojang, Fabric, Quilt, Forge and NeoForge servers: game versions, game files and mod loaders.
  • Modrinth: searching and downloading packs and content, and checking for updates by file hash.
  • CurseForge: searches and file lookups go through our proxy server, which adds the API key so that it never ships inside the launcher. The proxy forwards the request and does not store your profile data. Like most web servers it may keep short-lived technical logs such as IP address and time for abuse protection.

No sale of data

We do not sell personal data and we do not use advertising or tracking services in the launcher.

Your choices

You can sign out and delete profiles at any time. Uninstalling the launcher and deleting its folder in your user profile removes all locally stored data.
