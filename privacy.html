Privacy

This page explains what Hyperion Client does with your data. The short version is that almost everything stays on your own computer.

On your computer

Your profiles, settings, downloaded game files, logs and screenshots are stored in your Windows user folder. They are not uploaded anywhere. If you delete the launcher folder, all of it is gone.

Microsoft login

To check that you own Minecraft, the launcher uses the official Microsoft, Xbox and Minecraft sign-in. You type your password on a Microsoft page, never inside Hyperion Client. The tokens you get back are kept on your computer and are only used to confirm ownership and to start the game. I do not run a server that receives or stores them.

Other connections

The launcher talks to Mojang, Fabric, Quilt, Forge and NeoForge to download game files and loaders, and to Modrinth to search for and download content. CurseForge searches go through a small server of mine, because CurseForge requires an API key and I do not want to ship that key inside the app. That server only passes your search or file request on to CurseForge. Like most web servers it may keep technical logs for a short time, for example your IP address and the time of the request, to protect against abuse.

Selling and tracking

I do not sell any data and there is no advertising or analytics in the launcher.

Questions

If you want to know more or have a request about your data, open an issue on GitHub.

Hyperion Client is an independent project and has nothing to do with Mojang or Microsoft.
