You need Minecraft

Hyperion Client is free and has no in-app purchases. Minecraft is not included. A valid Minecraft: Java Edition license and valid authentication are required to play. Direct starts use official Microsoft authentication and ownership checks; the official-launcher path uses that launcher's independent account session.

Content from other people

Mods, modpacks and similar files come from authors on platforms like Modrinth and CurseForge. They have their own licenses and rules. Hyperion does not grant rights to redistribute third-party content. Some files may require a separate download or may be unavailable through a platform's API.

Social features and server rules

Online social features require sign-in and acceptance of the displayed community rules. Privacy controls, blocking, reporting and appeals are available in the launcher. Server operators set participation rules and may require a valid protected session before admitting a player.

Protected sessions

Hyperion Anti-cheat runs for a selected protected session after consent and administrator permission. Declining permission or failing required checks can prevent that protected start. Unprotected profiles do not activate it. The current implementation may delete identified x-ray resource or shader packs from the selected profile. Review the privacy notice before enabling protection.

Development status

Loader support depends on the selected Minecraft version. The in-game client mod is temporarily disabled. The project is in development and a Microsoft Store submission is being prepared.

No connection to Mojang or Microsoft

This is an independent project. It is not an official Minecraft product and it is not approved by Mojang or Microsoft. Minecraft is a trademark of Mojang Studios.

No guarantees

The launcher is provided as it is. Mods can break worlds, so make a backup of anything you care about before you install or update something or enable a protected session. Profile backups do not replace your own world backups.
